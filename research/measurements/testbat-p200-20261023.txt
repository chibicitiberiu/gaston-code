**[14] is the one to keep.** `E + msaa` at 268 ms against `D + text` at
25 ms is MSAA costing ~10x on the same 54 nodes, which corroborates the 3.4x

mind when reading any walk figure taken on the emulator.


=== The one failure, and it is hardware-only ===

  FAIL  steps-waitfor-times-out-at-its-own-timeout
        at=0 status=truncated took=309 ()

`research/guest/autocheck.cpp` asserts `at == 0 && status == "timeout" &&
took < 1500` for a `wait_for` on a selector that can never match, with a
300 ms step timeout. Two of the three hold: it failed at step 0 and it took
309 ms. The status is wrong -- **`truncated`, not `timeout`**.

`truncated` comes from `UIWAIT_TRUNCATED` in `win/automation/steps.cpp`:
the wait's tree walk hit its node cap before it could decide. On Wine's
near-empty desktop the walk never truncates and the case passes, which is
why 465 wine-smoke tests are green. On a real desktop with 61 top-level
windows it truncates every time.

**This matters beyond the test.** A caller waiting for something that never
appears is told `truncated`, which means "I could not see the whole desktop"
and invites raising the cap or retrying. The truth is "it is not there".
Those are different answers and a caller branching on status will take the
wrong one -- on the machine the product is for, and never on the machine its
tests run on. Recorded as BACKLOG 41.

It is NOT caused by the hidden-window change made the same day: the
`AUTOCHK.EXE` on this box is the 15 October kit build, and that change was
in `win/ui/uiengine.cpp`, which autocheck does not link.
