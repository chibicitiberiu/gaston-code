What the "agent is driving" indicator costs, before it does anything
====================================================================
2026-10-14, host build (Open Watcom), stage 4b commit 2 of N.

WHY THIS WAS THE FIRST THING BUILT
----------------------------------
The plan's own first risk was that the feature does not fit. gaston had
2,560 bytes of cap slack and gremote 3,072, and the review's arithmetic said
a single 4,096-byte _BSS page slide would exceed both on its own, before a
line of content -- so the shape was linked and measured before the behaviour
was written. What is in the image at this point: the class, its thread, its
window, its paint, its hotkey, and both programs owning one. Nothing raises
it; no drive calls it.

The second question was whether the seam holds. libautomation is linked by
uicheck, autocheck, keyprobe and focusprobe as well as by the two programs,
and wlink pulls a library member in on any symbol reference -- so if
anything in input.cpp or steps.cpp had named DriveIndicator directly, the
window and the thread would be in four images that can never raise it. That
is why DriveOverlay is abstract and header-only.

THE PAGE SLIDE DID NOT HAPPEN, AND THAT IS THE RESULT
-----------------------------------------------------
DGROUP's charged figure did not move on either program, although its FILE
size grew 1,536 bytes on gaston and 1,536 on gremote (the strings, the
vtables). The gate charges max(virtual, raw) and DGROUP is charged at its
virtual size, which carries the padding before _BSS's page-aligned base --
so this commit's data was absorbed by padding stage 4's own raise had
already bought. _BSS's base did not move. The feared +4,096 is not in these
numbers.

.idata behaved as the review predicted, in the one place it predicted:
gremote had never imported DrawEdge, DrawTextA, GetTextExtentPoint32A,
GetStockObject, FillRect, BeginPaint, EndPaint, InvalidateRect or
RedrawWindow, because before this it drew nothing at all -- 448 bytes, one
512-byte charge. gaston needed only SetWindowPos, RegisterHotKey and
UnregisterHotKey (102 bytes) and had 146 free, so it paid nothing.

THE SEAM HELD
-------------
  gaston.map     indicator.o present
  gremote.map    indicator.o present
  uicheck.map    absent
  autocheck.map  absent

RegisterHotKey and UnregisterHotKey passed tools/check/imports.py unchanged:
they are USER32 exports present since Win95, and the gate's deny-list is of
NT-only symbols, not an allow-list of names.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SAY
----------------------
Nothing here was run. The window has never been created, the thread has
never started, and the chord has never been registered -- on this host or on
the guest. The numbers are what the linker produced, and the behaviour is
the next commits' business.
