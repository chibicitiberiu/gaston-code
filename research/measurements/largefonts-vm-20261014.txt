The Options sheet at Large Fonts -- BACKLOG 30, stage 4
QEMU Win98 SE guest, 800x600, Large Fonts (120 dpi), 2026-10-14

WHY
  BACKLOG 30 listed this as "a measurement we do not have: 230x170 DLU was
  COMPUTED, not seen; the VM runs 800x600 small fonts and switching needs a
  reboot of the shared VM." A dialog laid out in DLUs is only as right as
  its arithmetic until somebody looks at it on a machine that scales them.

METHOD
  Snapshot first (vm/win98.qcow2.known-good-20261014-002539-pre-largefonts),
  because a wrong display setting can leave a guest that will not reach a
  desktop. Then Display Properties > Settings > Advanced > Font Size >
  Large Fonts, and a restart.

  TWO THINGS ABOUT THE RESTART, both already known and both worth repeating
  because they cost most of the time here. Win98 on this VM WEDGES at
  "Windows is now restarting..." and never resets -- the no-ACPI behaviour
  [[vm-needs-stop-then-start]] records -- so it has to be destroyed and
  started. And destroying it at that screen makes Windows boot into SAFE
  MODE the next time, which costs another clean shutdown and cold start.
  The working sequence is: apply, let it wedge, destroy, start (safe mode),
  shut down cleanly from safe mode, destroy, start.

WHAT THE SHEET LOOKS LIKE
  largefonts-permissions-20261014.png -- the densest page, and it HOLDS.
  Six tabs on one row, both Change files / Run programs columns, all eight
  dropdowns, the screen checkbox and OK/Cancel/Apply, inside 800x600. The
  "Ask timeout:" field sits tight against the right edge with a few pixels
  to spare, which is the narrowest thing on the sheet and is worth knowing
  before anything else is added to that row.

  Tools, Connection and the rest are comfortable. The 230x170 DLU figure
  stands.

AND IT FOUND A BUG, WHICH IS THE POINT OF LOOKING
  largefonts-addtool-clipped-20261014.png -- the Add-a-tool dialog this
  stage added (E13) had its OK and Cancel buttons CUT OFF by the bottom
  edge of the dialog.

  Not a Large Fonts problem at all: the dialog was declared 128 DLU tall
  and the buttons sit at y=119 with height 14, so they end at 133. It was
  clipped at every font size and nobody had seen it, because the gates
  cannot check a dialog template against its own contents and the guest
  check for E13 had only looked at the menu items.

  Fixed to 140 DLU. largefonts-addtool-fixed-20261014.png is the same
  dialog after: both buttons whole, with margin below.

LEFT AS FOUND
  The guest is returned to Small Fonts afterwards; the sheet's other
  consumers and every earlier measurement assume it.
