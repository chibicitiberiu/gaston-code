Q1. Can a process with no window take the foreground? YES
----------------------------------------------------------

Open since 2026-09-27 (REMOTE.md's P200 checklist, BACKLOG 16's residue).
98 introduced the foreground lock -- measured at 15000 ms -- and the worry
was that `SetForegroundWindow` from a background process would only flash a
taskbar button.

      before the step   Progman, SHELLDLL_DefView, SysListView32
      focus Notepad     ok, hit Notepad "Untitled - Notepad"
      after the step    Notepad, Edit

`Input::focus` relaxes SPI_SETFOREGROUNDLOCKTIMEOUT around the one call that
needs it and puts it back, and that is enough from a program with no window
of its own on the screen. The AttachThreadInput fallback was not needed.


Q2. Does retail 98 carry oleacc.dll? YES, and it answers properly
------------------------------------------------------------------

      HELLO msaa=1

asked from the reader thread by `rc_msaa_present` -- LoadLibrary,
GetProcAddress, FreeLibrary, no COM apartment anywhere near it.

This is a stronger answer than Wine's. Wine HAS oleacc and returns the
generic client object for every window class, so a green MSAA result there
proves only that the call did not fail. Here the walk comes back with real
roles and real names:

      roles   list, pagetablist, pushbutton, text, toolbar
      names   ToolbarWindow32 "Application", Button "Start"

so `[name=...]` and `[role=...]` selectors address what they say they do.


Q3. BACKLOG 26's number: 37 ms, which is below the cost of asking
------------------------------------------------------------------

A full MSAA-enriched walk of this desktop, hidden windows included:

      0.036 s, 0.037 s

and the request after a cancelled one is answered in 0.044 s.

26 guessed "on the Athlon box a desktop tree is tens of milliseconds and on
the Pentium 200 it is not". Tens of milliseconds, confirmed.

The honest consequence: a STOP cannot catch a walk here at all. Eight
threaded attempts, racing the stop against the walk from the moment the id
exists, caught none -- the walk finishes before the round trip lands. So the
problem 26 describes does not arise on this machine. The mechanism is
proved (the VM caught it on attempt 1, and STOP on a step list works here --
uilaneprobe 8a), and the motivation is still a slower box with a busier
desktop. This desktop was freshly booted: 16 to 18 visible windows.


Q4. What shouldCompress decides for a screenshot: IT COMPRESSES
----------------------------------------------------------------

A 1280x720 8bpp capture, 922,678 bytes, four alternating runs of three:

      deflate=1    0.046  0.087  0.046   best 0.046 s
      deflate=0    0.093  0.090  0.091   best 0.090 s
      deflate=1    0.046  0.046  0.084   best 0.046 s
      deflate=0    0.096  0.093  0.097   best 0.093 s

Byte-identical either way. Twice as fast compressed, repeatably.

THIS REVERSES WHAT THIS PROJECT PREDICTED. The plan for step 6 said, from
the VM, that `Connection::Codec::shouldCompress` -- `linkBps < deflateBps/2`
-- would decline on a LAN, "correctly, since 300KB raw at LAN speed beats
compressing it first", and `deflate-gremote-vm-20261003.txt` says the same.
On the emulator it did decline. On this hardware it does not: an Athlon XP
deflates fast enough at level 1 that half its throughput is still above what
100Mbit carries, so the comparison lands the other way and the screenshot
goes compressed.

The prediction was not wrong about the rule, it was wrong about the machine
it was made on -- which is the whole argument for this gate. A QEMU guest's
deflate throughput is the host's emulation speed, not a 1998 CPU's, and that
is exactly the number this heuristic turns on.


And the pid bug, on the hardware it was about
----------------------------------------------

`scope=pid` with a 9x process id -- an obfuscated pointer with the high bit
set, which no signed clamp can carry:

      NOTEPAD.EXE, pid 0xfffdf1e3   ->  2 nodes of the desktop's 18
                                        Notepad, Edit

Before the fix this answered with the whole desktop, silently, on the
shipping client as well as on gremote.
